# How to Update Stock from Purchase Orders

## ⚠️ Important: Stock Updates Require 2 Steps

### Step 1: Create Purchase Order
✅ You've done this already
- This just records that you ordered items
- Stock is NOT updated yet

### Step 2: Receive Stock (THIS IS WHAT YOU NEED!)
⭐ This is where stock gets updated
- When items physically arrive
- Click "Receive Stock" button
- Then inventory increases

---

## 📋 Complete Process:

### **1. Create Purchase Order**
```
Purchases → New PO → Add Items → Save
Status: "Pending"
Stock: Not changed yet ❌
```

### **2. Receive Stock (Updates Inventory)**
```
Purchases → Click PO → "📦 Receive Stock" button
Enter quantities → Click "Receive Stock"
Stock: NOW UPDATED ✅
```

## 💡 Why 2 Steps?

**Real World Example:**
1. **Monday**: You order 100 laptops from supplier → Create PO
2. **Friday**: Laptops arrive at warehouse → Receive Stock

Between Monday and Friday, you don't have the laptops yet!
So stock shouldn't update until Friday when you actually receive them.

---

## ✅ What Happens When You Receive Stock:

1. ✅ Product stock quantity increases
2. ✅ "Received" column updates
3. ✅ Stock movement record created
4. ✅ Order status changes to "Received"
5. ✅ You can now sell the items
