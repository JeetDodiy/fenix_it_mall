FENIX IT MALL — COMPLETE UI/UX REDESIGN & IMPROVEMENT INSTRUCTIONS

PROJECT CONTEXT
FENIX IT MALL is a Django-based IT and Computer Shop Management System. The existing project already contains:
- Accounts and role-based access: Admin, Manager, Cashier, Employee
- Dashboard with analytics/charts
- Products, categories, brands, product images, barcodes and QR codes
- Inventory with stock in/out, adjustments, damaged goods, low-stock and out-of-stock tracking
- Sales/POS with barcode scanner support, invoice generation, invoice editing, GST calculation and Cash/UPI/Card payments
- Purchase orders, receiving stock and supplier payments
- Customers and loyalty information
- Suppliers
- Employees, attendance and leave management
- Reports for sales, purchases, inventory, profit/loss, customers, suppliers and employees
- Notifications
- Company settings, logo and tax configuration
- Responsive design
- Light/dark theme
- Alpine.js, Chart.js, GSAP and Three.js
- Reusable base layout and components

IMPORTANT:
Do NOT rebuild the backend architecture unnecessarily.
Do NOT remove working features.
Do NOT break existing Django URLs, views, models, forms, permissions, database relationships or business logic unless a change is required for the improved UX.
The primary objective is to significantly improve the FRONTEND UI/UX and make the application feel like a real commercial IT retail/POS product.

==================================================
1. MAIN DESIGN OBJECTIVE
==================================================

Redesign FENIX IT MALL so that a real-life:
- Shop owner
- Store manager
- Cashier
- Inventory employee
- Sales employee

can open the system for the first time and immediately understand how to use it.

The first impression must be:

“Professional, modern, fast, simple, and designed for my shop.”

Do NOT make it look like a generic college CRUD project or a generic admin template.

Prioritize:
CLARITY > DECORATION
SPEED > COMPLEXITY
HUMAN WORKFLOW > DATABASE STRUCTURE
VISUAL HIERARCHY > INFORMATION OVERLOAD
PROFESSIONALISM > FLASHY EFFECTS
